About Mendyr

We're on a mission to make quality healthcare accessible to everyone, right in the comfort of their homes.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between patients and quality healthcare by providing a trusted platform that connects families with verified, compassionate healthcare professionals for at-home care.

Our Vision

A world where every person has access to professional healthcare services without leaving their home. We envision a future where home-based care is the standard, not the exception.

Our Values

Trust, transparency, compassion, and excellence guide everything we do. We believe in putting patients first while empowering healthcare professionals to do their best work.

Our Team

Mendyr is built by a passionate team of healthcare professionals, technologists, and entrepreneurs who share a common goal — making healthcare more human and accessible.

Connecting patients with verified nurses and caregivers for professional at-home healthcare services.
